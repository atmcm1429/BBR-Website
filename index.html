About Us

Bull & Bear Research (BBR) is Northeastern University’s equity research club, where members produce semesterly reports analyzing sectors such as technology, industrials, healthcare, and broader macroeconomic market trends. Through these reports, members conduct fundamental analysis, build valuation models, and develop investment theses on public companies and industries. The club provides students with hands-on experience in financial modeling, valuation, and equity research while fostering collaboration among students interested in careers in finance.
